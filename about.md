I am a political science student completing a Master's degree. My research sits at the intersection of public opinion, party strategy, and electoral behaviour in Scandinavia, with a focus on the Danish case.

My thesis tests Zaller's (1992) mainstreaming hypothesis: when political elites converge on a policy position, do citizens of the relevant parties follow? I study this through the lens of Danish immigration politics — specifically Socialdemokratiet's rightward shift on immigration policy between 2015 and 2019 — using four waves of the Danish National Election Study and expert-rated party positions from the Chapel Hill Expert Survey.

## Research Interests

- Public opinion formation and elite cue-taking
- Immigration politics and welfare chauvinism
- Scandinavian party systems and electoral behaviour
- Quantitative methods in political science
